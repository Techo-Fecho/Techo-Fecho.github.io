ADAU1787 Feedforward ANC Headset – SigmaStudio POC

0:38

Developed a software-side proof of concept for a one-ear feedforward ANC headset using the Analog Devices ADAU1787 DSP. Built the SigmaStudio signal chain from the analog feedforward microphone through the DSP to the headset driver, including ANC/bypass routing, signal splitting, safe gain staging, polarity-aware routing, and a FIR-based ANC structure. The POC was compiled and prepared for hardware bring-up and acoustic validation.
