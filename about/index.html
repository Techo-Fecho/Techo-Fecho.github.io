Our Story

From ring zero outward.

Haeman Tech was founded on a single conviction: the hardest, most valuable engineering work happens below the surface — in the Windows kernel, in bare-metal firmware, in the device communication protocols that move data between a microcontroller and the world.

Most software agencies stop at the application layer. We don't. We write the Windows kernel drivers, the embedded firmware, and the system software that makes hardware actually work — and we ship it to production, signed, certified, and stable.

Today we're a full deep-tech engineering team serving clients globally, from enterprise security vendors to hardware startups, on problems that demand genuine systems expertise in Windows kernel development, embedded systems, and hardware-software integration.
